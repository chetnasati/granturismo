THE GENEVA FOUNDING

Like There Is No Tomorrow.

Gran Turismo Events was born in Geneva in 2002 with a singular, unwavering conviction: supercars are built to be driven. Not stored away under silk covers in air-conditioned vaults, but piloted through mountain passes, hearing naturally aspirated V12s reverberate against granite cliffs, and feeling the precise turn-in of a machine engineered to perform at its peak.

What began twenty-four years ago as an intimate alpine road trip organized by Peter Ternström and a tight circle of driving companions has grown into the world’s benchmark private automotive fraternity. Across two decades, we have organized more than 180 rallies across 35 countries—from the highest peaks of the Swiss and Austrian Alps to the closed curves of the Nürburgring Nordschleife, the sunlit switchbacks of the Amalfi Coast, and the royal yacht berths of Port Hercule in Monaco.

We do not organize public parades, commercial rallies, or chaotic street spectacles. Every Gran Turismo departure is private, strictly capped in vehicle numbers, and curated turn by turn. Lifelong friendships are forged over empty morning mountain switchbacks, shared track telemetry, and Michelin-starred evening tables.

“There is nothing better in life than a proper road trip with great roads, fantastic locations, and good friends.”
— Peter Ternström, Founder

THE GRAN TURISMO STANDARD

How We Curate Every Journey

01

Scouted Tarmac

Every pass, hairpin, and rest stop is personally driven in advance. We calculate fuel ranges, road surface condition, and timing so you experience the road with absolute flow and freedom.

02

Iconic Venues

From Villa d'Este on Lake Como to The Chedi Andermatt, Hôtel Byblos Saint-Tropez, and private Tuscan castello estates—our accommodations are the world's standard in discreet luxury.

03

Supercars Only

Strict vehicle criteria preserve group pace and dynamic cohesion. Whether piloting your own Porsche GT3 RS, Ferrari 296, or McLaren, every vehicle belongs on an alpine stage.

04

Rally Support

Dedicated reconnaissance pace cars, door-to-door luggage van transport, professional motorsport photographers, and encrypted private comms ensure effortless peace of mind.
