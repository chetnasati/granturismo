HERITAGE & COMMUNITY

Driven by Passion. Since 2002.

Bringing together a community of supercar enthusiasts through extraordinary driving experiences.

Gran Turismo Events was born in Geneva in 2002 with a singular, unwavering philosophy: supercars are built to be driven on the world's most magnificent roads, in the company of people who share the same infectious devotion.

What began twenty-four years ago as an intimate road rally across the Swiss passes has matured into the world’s benchmark private automotive fraternity. We do not organize public parades or commercial spectacles; we curate private, deeply authentic driving adventures where lifelong friendships are forged over mountain switchbacks and Michelin tables.

Every departure is restricted to an exclusive group of drivers. Every route is personally scouted turn by turn. Every accommodation is chosen for genuine character and discretion. This is our standard—driven by passion, like there is no tomorrow.
