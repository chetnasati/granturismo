# Gran Turismo Events

> **The Art of Driving.**  
> Exceptional roads. Iconic circuits. Extraordinary experiences.  
> Driven by passion since 2002.

A clean, minimal, and ultra-luxurious web platform for **Gran Turismo Events**, designed with an architectural editorial aesthetic, typography pairing display serif (`Apris` / `Cormorant Garamond`) and grotesque sans-serif (`PP Neue Montreal` / `Plus Jakarta Sans`), generous whitespace, and restrained brand accents.

---

## Brand Architecture & Sections

1. **Homepage Hero:** Fullscreen cinematic supercar cinematography with minimal text and prominent CTAs.
2. **Experiences:** Three signature driving formats:
   - Supercar Driving Tours (daily vehicle rotation)
   - Iconic Circuits & Grand Prix Hospitality (Monaco GP Superyacht, Goodwood, Spa & Nürburgring)
   - Tailor-Made Private Odysseys
3. **Upcoming Events (`#calendar`):** Spacious, image-led event cards with clear dates, destinations, vehicle allocations, and availability.
4. **Beyond the Drive (`#beyond-the-drive`):** Large destination photography, detailed day-by-day itinerary breakdown, and journey specifications.
5. **Le Shoppe (`#shoppe`):** The Gran Turismo Drivers Collection, featuring timepieces (Sjöö & Sandström, Halda Space Race Polonia), custom-embroidered driver jackets, apparel, and leather travel goods sourced from [`shop.granturismoevents.com`](https://shop.granturismoevents.com).
6. **About Us (`#our-story`):** Brand heritage and community statistics celebrating over 24 years since 2002.
7. **The Journal (`#journal`):** Editorial road dispatches and driver perspectives.
8. **Contact (`#contact`):** Minimal enquiry form and 24/7 global concierge representation in Geneva, Monaco, and London.

## Pages

- [`index.html`](index.html) — Main portal with hero, experiences, calendar, itinerary showcase, Le Shoppe, heritage, journal, and concierge desk.
- [`fleet.html`](fleet.html) — Dedicated supercar fleet showcase (Ferrari 296 GTB, Porsche 911 GT3 RS, McLaren 750S Spider, Aston Martin DB12) and rotation system.
- [`destinations.html`](destinations.html) — Catalog of world-class driving regions (Swiss Alps, Dolomites, French Riviera, Tuscany, Highlands).
- [`experiences.html`](experiences.html) — In-depth tour formats and VIP motorsport hospitality.
- [`journey-planner.html`](journey-planner.html) — 4-step interactive concierge custom itinerary designer wizard.

## Getting Started

Serve locally using any standard static web server:

```bash
# Python 3 built-in HTTP server
python3 -m http.server 8000
```

Open [http://localhost:8000](http://localhost:8000) in your web browser.
